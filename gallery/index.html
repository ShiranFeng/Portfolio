Gallery

A collection of course projects, personal illustrations, sketches, and visual experiments.

This gallery brings together work created both in and beyond the classroom. Some pieces began with a design brief or assignment, while others grew from everyday observations, imagined stories, and questions I wanted to explore visually.

Across these works, I experiment with storytelling, character, colour, composition, and the relationship between people and their surroundings.
